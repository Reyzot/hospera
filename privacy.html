Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Hosperai ("we", "us") is operated by Andreu Rey, Miami Beach, Florida. We help hotels, clinics and local businesses ("Businesses") thank their customers after a visit or stay, ask them for a review, and reply to the reviews they receive. This policy explains what data we handle and how.

1. Who this applies to

  • Businesses that sign up for Hosperai (our clients).
  • Customers of those Businesses (for example hotel guests or clinic patients) whose name and contact details the Business gives us so we can send them messages on its behalf.
  • Visitors to hosperai.es and app.hosperai.es.

2. Data we collect

  • From Businesses: business name, address, contact person, email, WhatsApp/phone number, Google and Tripadvisor listing links, reply-style preferences and billing details.
  • About their customers: first name, mobile number and/or email, preferred language, visit or stay dates, and whether they are a VIP member. Businesses enter this information themselves and must have obtained it lawfully from their customers.
  • Interaction data: whether a message was delivered and whether a review link was opened, so we don't send unnecessary reminders.
  • Public review data: reviews published on Google and Tripadvisor about the Business.
  • Website data: basic analytics (Google Analytics) and the information you submit in our forms.

3. How we use it

  • To send the Business's customers a thank-you message after their visit or stay with links to leave a review, an optional check-in during longer stays, and one reminder email if they haven't opened the review link.
  • To notify the Business of new reviews and draft suggested replies with the help of AI. The Business decides what is published.
  • To produce reports for the Business (review trends, Google Maps position, competitor reviews).
  • To provide support, bill for the service and improve it.

We ask every customer for a review in the same way. We never filter customers by satisfaction, never write, buy or incentivise fake reviews, and never post reviews on anyone's behalf.

4. SMS and WhatsApp messaging

Customers receive messages because they provided their mobile number to the Business at booking, check-in or visit, and were informed they would receive a thank-you message. Messages are transactional and related to their visit or stay. Message frequency varies (typically 1–3 messages per visit). Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to any SMS to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. You can also email andreu@gethosperai.com. If you reply to our WhatsApp number, you'll receive the Business's direct contact details.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We only share it with the service providers we need to run Hosperai, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Twilio and Meta (WhatsApp Business) to deliver WhatsApp and SMS messages.
  • Zoho Mail and Google (Gmail) to send emails.
  • Cloudflare to host our website and store check-in data.
  • Anthropic (Claude) to draft review replies and translations. Only the review text and the Business's style preferences are sent, never customer contact details.
  • SerpApi to read public Google review data.
  • Our payment provider to process subscriptions.

We may also disclose data if required by law.

6. How long we keep it

  • Customer contact details entered by a Business are deleted automatically 30 days after the visit or stay ends.
  • Business account data is kept while the subscription is active and deleted within 90 days after cancellation, except what we must keep for tax or legal reasons.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each Business's check-in link is private and signed, so one Business cannot see another's customers. Access to our systems is restricted to the Hosperai team.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop sending you messages, by emailing andreu@gethosperai.com. If you are a customer of a Business, we may forward your request to that Business, which is responsible for the data it gave us. Residents of the EU/UK and California have the rights granted by the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA respectively.

9. Children

Hosperai is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be communicated to Businesses by email.
